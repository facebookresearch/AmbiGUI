If the instruction is ambiguous — for example a missing size or date, or which of several targets is meant — send a message to the user with `send_msg_to_user` asking for what is missing, instead of guessing. The user is available throughout the task and will answer your messages; asking them a question is always allowed. Before your first action, reread the instruction and check whether it leaves open any choice that would change the outcome — which item or option, size, colour, date, time, location, quantity, or price range, or which of several matching results is meant. If it does, ask about it before you start.
